# Insurance-evidence ambiguity: uniformly excluded from headline scores

Decision recorded during first-pass execution, after Cedar Landing responses were reviewed. No sources, prompts, keys or runs are changed. The original 40-field results remain available, alongside this transparent sensitivity correction.

The property plan states that an insurer renewal confirmation exists and supplies a premium. It does not include the underlying insurer letter. The question asks for a **verified** premium. One reasonable reading accepts the statement in the plan; another distinguishes that statement from direct insurer evidence. Opus 5 explicitly made that distinction. Penalizing that caution would reward an ambiguity in the benchmark.

The `verified_renewal_insurance` criterion is therefore excluded from the headline extraction score for **every system, every case and both tracks**, regardless of its answer. Headline objective checks are 17 extraction plus 22 financial checks per case. The original criterion, expected value, response, strict grade and qualifications are still published as a diagnostic. No credit is awarded or deducted in narrative review solely for either reasonable interpretation of this indirect evidence. Inventing a premium absent from the packet, or failing to label Meadow Commerce's provisional allowance, remains an assessable unsupported claim under the frozen rubric.

The next case version should include an actual fictional insurer confirmation document or explicitly ask for the plan-reported premium. It must be frozen and rerun for every system before making a stronger claim about verification quality. This release does not establish insurance-verification superiority.
