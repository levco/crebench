# Ownership, openness and corrections

Created and published by Lev. Lev funds and develops this project and may participate in its product comparisons. This is not presented as an independent evaluation of Lev. External reviewer identities and conflicts will be published when appointed; none are represented as appointed in 0.1.0.

Original code, documentation and the fictional public example are MIT licensed. Keep third-party licenses attached to any later imports. Customer documents and protected keys are not included. The public repository should contain only material explicitly cleared for release.

Before the scored release, publish the protocol and eligible cohort. Commit to reporting the complete eligible result set, including unfavorable findings. Predetermined exceptions cover invalid studies and confidentiality incidents and require a public status explanation without exposing protected data. Sponsor review can correct facts and identify confidentiality issues; it cannot selectively alter scores or remove valid unfavorable results.

Anyone can reproduce the public example. Hidden evaluation verification will require an independent custodian or controlled reruns; that process must be implemented before claims based on protected tests. Hashes bind artifacts to a release; they are not proof of correctness or confidentiality. Preserve historical releases and publish every correction with its impact across all affected systems.

Version 0.1.0 contains no model results. Reference-answer checks are software validation only. No claim of superiority, professional parity or production readiness follows from them.
