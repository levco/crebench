Executive Summary
Source-backed analysis. Assumptions remain subject to diligence.
Beacon Plaza is a 12-suite, 66,360 SF office asset in Charlotte, NC. The property manager's certified rent roll
dated 2026-08-31 controls physical occupancy: nine suites (49,770 SF) are in active possession, one suite
(010, 5,380 SF) is signed but pending possession, and two suites (011 and 012) are vacant. Physical
occupancy is therefore 75.00% by unit count and 75.00% by area, not the 10-of-12 (83.3%) 'leased' headline in
the manager cover note, which counts the pending occupant that has not taken possession.
Underwritten per the acquisition analysis instructions on all-suite scheduled rent of $1,618,800 (12 suites x
contractual monthly rent x 12) less a 5% economic loss, plus $350 per suite of other income, effective gross
income is $1,542,060. After taxes of $169,974, insurance of $56,658, other recurring expenses of $226,632
and a 3% management fee, underwritten NOI before reserves is $1,042,534 and net cash flow after $300 per
suite of replacement reserves is $1,038,934. Capitalized at 7.5000%, the indicated value is $13,900,456.
Debt sizing at a 6.7500% sizing rate (the greater of the 6.7500% note rate and the 6.00% floor) over a 30-year
amortization produces a maximum loan of $8,340,274, bound by the 60.00% LTV test. After the 1% origination
fee ($83,403), $45,000 of closing costs and the $6,500,000 existing payoff, net cash-out to the sponsor is
approximately $1,711,871. No asking price, photographs, market sales or rent comparables, or sponsor track
record are supplied in the packet, and none have been assumed or created.

Risks and Missing Diligence
Occupancy presentation: the manager's 10-of-12 headline includes Suite 010, which has not taken possession.
This overstates physical occupancy by 8.3 percentage points; the certified detail governs and has been used
here.
Near-term rollover: Suite 001 (5,380 SF, $132,600 annual rent) expires 2027-02-28, inside the twelve-month
window. Renewal terms, tenant credit and downtime assumptions are not supplied.
Lease-up dependency: the prescribed underwriting applies a 5% economic loss to all-suite scheduled rent while
25% of the area is not in active possession. Underwritten NOI is therefore stabilized and above the trailing
actual; achieving it requires possession by Suite 010 and lease-up of Suites 011 and 012 at roll rents.
Sensitivity to this gap is not covered by the 5% loss factor alone.
