Executive Summary
Magnolia Terrace is a 48-unit, 40,800 square foot multifamily property in Raleigh, North Carolina, offered for
investment/sale with an accompanying financing analysis. The revised certified rent roll dated September 1,
2026 controls. Forty-five suites are in active possession, no suites are pending, and three suites (046, 047, 048)
are vacant, giving unit occupancy of 0.9375 and area occupancy of 0.9375.
Underwritten effective gross income is $945,045: all-unit scheduled monthly rent on the revised roll of $81,425
per month annualized to $977,100 of gross potential rent, less a 5% economic loss, plus other income of $350
per unit per year ($16,800). Underwritten operating expenses of $301,183 (taxes $102,312, insurance $34,104,
other recurring $136,416, management fee of 3% of EGI $28,351) produce underwritten NOI before reserves of
$643,862. After $300 per unit of replacement reserves ($14,400), underwritten net cash flow is $629,462.
Capitalizing NOI before reserves at the revised 6.0000% rate indicates a capitalization value of $10,731,028.
Sizing at 65% LTV, 1.25x minimum DSCR on NCF and 9% minimum NCF debt yield, over a 30-year monthly
amortization at the revised 7.0000% sizing rate, produces a maximum loan of $6,307,523, still constrained by
the DSCR test. After a 1% origination fee ($63,075), $45,000 of closing costs and the $4,200,000 existing
payoff, net cash-out is $1,999,448, so there is no refinance shortfall.
No asking price, photographs, market sale or rent comparables, or sponsor history are supplied in the packet;
their absence is disclosed rather than estimated. Source-backed analysis. Assumptions remain subject to
diligence.

Sensitivity
The revised workbook carries a live grid of maximum loan across cap rates of 5.50% to 6.50% and sizing rates
of 6.50% to 8.00%, holding revised NOI and NCF constant. DSCR governs throughout the revised grid, so
proceeds are driven by the sizing rate rather than by value at these cap rates.
Indicative maximum loan at the 6.00% cap rate: approximately $6,639,000 at a 6.50% sizing rate, $6,307,523 at
the base 7.00%, $6,002,000 at 7.50% and $5,719,000 at 8.00%. Each 50 bp move in the sizing rate shifts
proceeds by roughly $300,000, which flows dollar for dollar (net of the 1% fee) into net cash-out. Figures
recompute live in the workbook.
