Executive Summary
Source-backed analysis. Assumptions remain subject to diligence.
This revision applies the revision notice and the revised certified rent roll dated 2026-09-01, which supersedes
the 2026-08-31 certified roll. Three items changed: Suite 002 is confirmed active with scheduled monthly rent
increased by $225 to $7,025; the initial sizing rate is increased by 75 basis points to 7.2500%; and the
capitalization rate is increased by 25 basis points to 7.0000%. All other lease details, underwriting rules,
expense inputs, loan constraints, fees and the $4,300,000 payoff remain unchanged, and the prior executed
Suite 001 amendment continues to apply. The prior deliverables (underwriting-v1.xlsx and
memorandum-v1.pdf) remain in the audit archive and are not replaced.
Maple Crossing is a ten-suite retail property in Columbus, Ohio, with 32,150 rentable square feet across three
suite groups (Group A / Small, Group B / Standard, Group C / Large). Nine suites totaling 29,070 SF are in
physical possession and Suite 010 (3,080 SF, Group A) is vacant: unit occupancy 0.9000 and area occupancy
0.9042, unchanged from the prior version. Every active lease expires 2029-08-31, so there is no rollover within
the next twelve months.
Revised underwriting produces gross potential rent of $821,400, EGI of $783,830, NOI before reserves of
$531,078 and net cash flow of $528,078 after the $300-per-suite replacement reserve. Capitalized at 7.0000%,
NOI before reserves indicates a value of $7,586,830 — lower than the prior $7,830,964 because the 25 bp
cap-rate increase outweighs the $2,488 NOI gain. Debt sizing at 7.2500% yields an LTV limit of $4,931,440
(binding), a DSCR limit of $5,160,720 and a debt yield limit of $5,867,534, for a maximum loan of $4,931,440.
After the 1% origination fee ($49,314), $45,000 of closing costs and the $4,300,000 payoff, net cash-out is
$537,125 — still positive, so no additional sponsor equity is required and there is no refinance shortfall.
No asking price, photographs, market sales or rent comparables, appraisal, tenant credit information or sponsor
history is supplied in the packet. These remain explicitly unknown and are not estimated.

Lease and Occupancy Analysis
Nine of ten suites are in possession; Suite 010 remains vacant. The manager's cover note headlines 9 of 10 as
leased by counting occupied plus pending occupants and discloses that pending occupants have not taken
possession. The revised certified detail, which controls physical occupancy, again shows no suite in a
pending-possession status, so the headline coincides numerically with nine occupied suites while the
methodology itself remains a disclosed overstatement practice to monitor.
Two superseded rolls are in the packet: the 2026-06-30 archive roll (stale) and the 2026-08-31 certified roll
(superseded by the revision notice). Conflicts are resolved by date in favour of the 2026-09-01 revised roll; the
older rolls are retained only for version comparison.
Suite 001's executed lease sets base rent at $6,650 per month on 3,080 SF with a $13,300 security deposit and
no free-rent concession as of the evaluation date. The signed amendment (both parties executed 2026-07-20,
effective 2026-08-01) adds $350 per month and, per the revision notice, continues to apply, so Suite 001
in-place rent is $7,000 per month. There is no unsigned rent proposal in the packet. Annual in-place rent for
occupied suites is $745,800, up $2,700 from the prior version on the Suite 002 increase.
Per instruction, underwritten gross potential uses all-suite scheduled rent on the controlling roll ($68,450 per
month x 12 = $821,400) before the Suite 001 amendment; the amendment affects in-place rent only.
