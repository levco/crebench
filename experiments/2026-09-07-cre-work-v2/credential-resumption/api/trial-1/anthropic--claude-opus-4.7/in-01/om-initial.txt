Executive Summary
Source-backed analysis. Assumptions remain subject to diligence.
Falcon Distribution is an 8-suite industrial asset totaling 176,060 SF in Indianapolis. Seven suites are physically
occupied (154,060 SF) and one suite (008) is vacant on the manager's certified rent roll dated 2026-08-31. The
manager's cover characterizes occupancy as 7 of 8 by including pending occupants that have not taken
possession; the certified detail controls physical occupancy at 7 of 8 active only if suite 008 is treated as vacant.
Certified detail governs.
Prescribed underwriting produces EGI of $1,335,320, NOI (before reserves) of $902,476 and NCF of $900,076.
Capitalized at 6.2500%, gross value indication is $14,439,622. Sized on the 7.00% floor (greater of note rate
6.10% and floor 7.00%) at 30-year amortization with 1.25x DSCR / 9% NCF debt yield / 65% LTV, the DSCR
test binds at approximately $9,019,220. After 1% origination, $45,000 of closing costs and a $7,800,000
existing payoff, net cash-out to the sponsor is approximately $1,084,027.
Photos, asking price, market sales and rent comparables, and sponsor history are not included in the packet
and have not been inferred.

Risks & Missing Diligence
Present risks: (1) manager includes pending, unpossessed units in occupancy headline; (2) manager's
historical ledger appears to be clean of capital-in-opex — the roof replacement is booked separately as capital,
not in repairs, so the recurring-expense line does not embed a capital cost — this specific risk is therefore
absent as reported.
Unresolved/absent risks: near-term rollover is absent because all leases expire 2029-08-31, beyond a
12-month horizon; unsigned rent change is absent because no unsigned rent proposal is included in the packet
(lease-correspondence.pdf confirms no amendments affecting suite 001); temporary free rent is absent per
lease-001.pdf; sizing floor binding is present because the 7.00% floor exceeds the 6.10% note rate; refinance
shortfall is absent (net cash-out is positive $1,084,027); missing insurance is absent — insurance.pdf provides
GL policy declaration.
Missing diligence explicitly not supplied: photos, asking price, market sales/rent comps, sponsor history,
legal/environmental/property-condition/title reports (routine pending).
