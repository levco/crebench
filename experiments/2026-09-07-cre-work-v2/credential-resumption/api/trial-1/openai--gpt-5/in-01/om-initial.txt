Executive summary
As of 2026-08-31, Falcon Distribution is an 8-suite industrial property in Indianapolis, IN. The certified rent roll
shows 7 occupied and 1 vacant suite, totaling 176,060 SF with 87.5% occupancy by units and 87.5% by area.
Underwritten stabilized income is based on all-suite scheduled rent from the certified roll, less 5% economic
loss, plus $350 per suite of other income, per instructions.
Underwritten NOI before reserves is $902,612; NCF after reserves is $900,212. Capitalized at 6.25%, the
indicated value is $14.44 million. Debt sizing at the 7.00% floor and 30-year amortization yields a maximum
loan of about $9.02 million; after 1% origination, $45k closing costs, and a $7.80 million payoff, the transaction
produces approximately $1.09 million net cash out.
