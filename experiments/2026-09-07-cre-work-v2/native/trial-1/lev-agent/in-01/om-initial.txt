Executive summary
Falcon Distribution is an 8-suite industrial distribution asset totaling 176,060 SF in Indianapolis. As of the
2026-08-31 property-manager certified rent roll, 7 suites are actively leased and one suite (008) is vacant,
yielding 87.50% unit and 87.50% area occupancy. All active leases run through 2029-08-31, placing no
expirations inside the next 12 months. The trailing-12-month operating statement for the period ending 2026-08-31
reports collections of $1,262,520 and reported NOI of $869,736 (cash basis; no management fee booked; roof
replacement capitalized outside repairs, not recurring).
Applying the prescribed underwriting convention (all-suite scheduled monthly rent on the current certified roll
before the Suite 001 amendment, times 12; 5% economic loss; $350/unit other income; prescribed opex plus a 3%
management fee and $300/unit reserves), we underwrite EGI of $1,334,605, NOI (before reserves) of $901,783,
and NCF (after reserves) of $899,383. Capitalized at the prescribed 6.2500% cap rate, capitalization value is
$14,428,526.
Under the prescribed sizing framework (max LTV 65%, min DSCR 1.25×, min NCF debt yield 9%, 30-year monthly
amortization, sizing at the greater of the 6.10% note rate and the 7.00% floor), the maximum loan is $9,012,270,
with DSCR the binding constraint. After the $7,800,000 existing payoff, $45,000 closing costs, and a 1%
origination fee (~$90,123), the transaction produces positive net cash-out of $1,077,147 to the sponsor — no
refinance shortfall.
