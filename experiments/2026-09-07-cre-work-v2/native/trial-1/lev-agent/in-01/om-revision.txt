Executive summary
Falcon Distribution is an 8-suite, 176,060 SF industrial asset in Indianapolis. Per the revised certified rent roll as
of 2026-09-01, 7 suites are active and Suite 008 remains vacant → 87.50% unit and 87.50% area occupancy
(unchanged). All active leases run through 2029-08-31; no rollover within 12 months. Trailing-12-month reported
NOI is $869,736 (historical is not restated by the revision).
Applying the revised roll and unchanged expense conventions: EGI $1,337,170, NOI (pre-reserve) $904,271, NCF
$901,871. Capitalized at the revised 6.5000% → Value $13,911,860. Sizing at the revised 7.75% (floor still binding
vs. 6.10% note rate) over 30-yr monthly amortization produces Maximum loan $8,392,476, with DSCR the
binding constraint. After $7.8M payoff, $45K closing, and 1% origination (~$83,925), net cash-out is +$463,551
— reduced from v1 but still positive.
