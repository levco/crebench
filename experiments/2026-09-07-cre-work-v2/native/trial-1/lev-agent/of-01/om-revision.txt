1. Executive Summary (v2)
Beacon Plaza is a 12-suite (66,360 rentable SF) multi-tenant office property in Charlotte, NC. The revised
certified rent roll dated September 1, 2026 supersedes the prior roll. Ten suites are now in physical possession
(active) — Suite 010 has moved from pending to active with a $225 rent increase to $11,275 monthly — and two
suites remain vacant. Physical occupancy is 83.33% on units and 83.11% on area.
Applying the prescribed underwriting to the revised roll: GPR $1,620,600 (all-suite scheduled monthly on the new
roll, Suite 001 pre-amendment, × 12); UW EGI $1,543,770; operating expenses $499,577; UW NOI (before
reserves) $1,044,193; UW NCF (after reserves) $1,040,593. Capitalized at the revised 7.75% cap on NOI:
concluded valuation $13,473,457.
Debt sized against 60% LTV / 1.25× DSCR / 9.0% DY at the revised 7.50% sizing rate (30-year monthly
amortization) yields a maximum loan of $8,084,074, LTV-bound. Net cash-out after 1% origination, $45,000
closing, and the $6,500,000 payoff (unchanged) is +$1,458,233. The transaction still does not require additional
sponsor equity at these terms. Asking price, photos, market sales and rent comps, and sponsor history remain
not supplied.
