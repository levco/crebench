Lease & Occupancy Analysis
The certified rent roll (as of 2026-08-31) shows 9 active suites and 1 vacant suite (Suite 010). All active leases share a
stated lease expiration of 2029-08-31 — no active suite expires within the next 12 months, so there is no near-term
rollover risk. The manager cover headline states 9 of 10 leased using a methodology that counts occupied plus pending
as leased; the certified detail — which controls per the manager's own instruction — shows no pending status on the roll.
The headline's classification methodology is flagged (see Risks) because it would overstate occupancy in any period
where pending occupants had not taken possession, even though the two figures happen to reconcile in this snapshot.
Suite 001 is subject to a fully executed lease amendment dated 2026-07-20 and effective 2026-08-01, raising monthly
rent by $350; the certified roll reflects the post-amendment rent of $6,650 at Suite 001. The lease excerpt (lease-001.pdf)
also shows $6,650, while the archive roll dated 2026-06-30 shows $6,575. This is a resolvable source conflict: the
executed later amendment controls per the lease excerpt itself, so the certified value stands. Per underwriting
instructions, underwriting uses Suite 001's pre-amendment rent ($6,300) as an assumption input to the stabilized GPR —
a deliberate conservative treatment, not a data error.
