Lease & Occupancy Analysis
The revised certified rent roll (as of 2026-09-01) shows 9 active suites and 1 vacant suite (Suite 010). All active leases
share a stated lease expiration of 2029-08-31 — no active suite expires within the next 12 months, so there is no
near-term rollover risk. The manager cover headline states 9 of 10 leased using a methodology that counts occupied
plus pending as leased; the certified detail — which controls per the manager's own instruction — shows no pending
status on the roll. The headline's classification methodology continues to be flagged (see Risks).
Suite 001 remains subject to the fully executed amendment dated 2026-07-20 and effective 2026-08-01 (+$350/mo). The
revised roll continues to reflect the post-amendment Suite 001 rent of $6,650. Per underwriting instructions preserved by
the revision notice, UW GPR uses Suite 001's pre-amendment rent ($6,300) as an assumption input — a deliberate
conservative treatment, not a data error.
Suite 002 is revised on the new roll to $7,025 monthly rent (+$225 vs. the prior roll). This is an authorized revision to the
certified roll, delivered as a signed instruction; there is no unsigned rent proposal in the packet.
