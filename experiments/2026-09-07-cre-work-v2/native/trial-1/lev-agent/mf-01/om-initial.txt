1. Executive Summary
Magnolia Terrace is a 48-unit multifamily property in Raleigh, NC. The property manager's certified rent roll as of
2026-08-31 controls, showing 44 units in active possession, 1 unit pending (Suite 045, tenant not yet in possession)
and 3 vacant units (Suites 046–048). Physical occupancy is 91.67% on a unit basis and 89.90% on an area basis.
The manager cover note headline of “leased 45 of 48” treats the pending unit as leased; this OM presents
possession-based physical occupancy separately.
Under the prescribed underwriting rules, gross potential rent equals all-suite scheduled rent on the certified roll × 12
= $974,400 (before the signed Suite 001 amendment). Applying a 5% economic loss, $350/unit/year of other
income, 3% management, and $300/unit reserves produces UW NOI of $641,374 (pre-reserves) and UW NCF of
$626,974 (post-reserves). At the prescribed 5.75% cap rate, the capitalized value is $11,154,323.
Sizing debt on the greater of the 6.25% note rate or 6.00% floor (note binds), 30-year amortization, and the
prescribed constraints (65% LTV, 1.25× DSCR, 9% debt yield on NCF), the maximum loan is $6,788,545, with
DSCR the binding constraint. After the $4,200,000 existing payoff, 1% origination and $45,000 closing costs, the
net cash-out is approximately $2,475,660 (positive). No refinance shortfall.

this OM. The archive roll dated 2026-06-30 shows older, lower rents and is treated as superseded per

rollover within the 12-month horizon.

Suite 001 rent — resolvable source conflict. Lease-001.pdf executes at $1,500/month; the signed
correspondence amendment (both parties executed 2026-07-20, effective 2026-08-01) increases monthly rent by
$350 to $1,850 in place. The certified rent roll (as of 2026-08-31) still shows $1,500 for Suite 001, which is the
pre-amendment figure. The signed amendment controls per lease-001.pdf's own language. Per the prescribed
underwriting rules, gross potential rent is calculated on the certified roll before the Suite 001 amendment, so this
reconciles: the amendment changes in-place rent only, not the UW GPR base. This is a known, explained source
conflict — not left unresolved.
