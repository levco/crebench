1. Executive Summary
Magnolia Terrace is a 48-unit multifamily property in Raleigh, NC. Under the revised certified rent roll as of
2026-09-01 (which supersedes the 2026-08-31 roll per revision-notice.pdf), the property is now 45 units in active
possession, 0 pending, and 3 vacant (Suites 046–048). Physical occupancy is 93.75% by unit and 92.43% by
area. The manager's headline 'leased 45 of 48' now aligns with possession-based occupancy — there is no longer a
pending-in-occupancy overstatement.
Applying the revised roll, gross potential rent is $977,100 (Suite 045 rent +$225/month). Underwriting assumptions
are unchanged except the two rate adjustments: cap rate 6.00% (+25 bps) and sizing rate 7.00% (+75 bps, on the
greater-of note-or-floor test — note still binds; floor of 6.00% is not binding). UW NOI is $643,862 (pre-reserves) and
UW NCF is $629,462 (post-reserves). Capitalized value at 6.00% is $10,731,028.
The maximum loan is $6,307,523, with DSCR still the binding constraint. After the $4,200,000 existing payoff,
1% origination and $45,000 closing costs, the net cash-out is $1,999,448 — positive; no refinance shortfall. Vs
v1, the loan is $481K smaller and net cash-out is $476K smaller, driven primarily by the +75 bps sizing rate.
